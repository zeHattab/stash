Stash is a free, open-source password and document manager that works entirely on your iPhone.

No account. No internet. No tracking. Your data is encrypted on the device and never leaves it — Stash makes no network calls at all.

WHAT IT DOES
• Passwords and logins, with a strong password generator and password history
• Two-factor codes (TOTP) with a live timer; add them by scanning a QR code, picking a screenshot, or typing a secret
• AutoFill in Safari and apps — both passwords and one-time codes
• Documents: passports, IDs, insurance and more, with an on-device scanner that reads the passport machine-readable zone and fills fields for you
• Expiry reminders for documents — local notifications with neutral text
• Search across everything

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline; nothing is ever uploaded
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers
• Encryption uses Apple's standard CryptoKit and CommonCrypto
• Face ID or Touch ID to unlock

EXTRA PROTECTION
• A recovery key, in case you forget your master password
• A second password that opens a separate, decoy vault — useful if you are ever forced to unlock under pressure

Free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Open source under the GPLv3 license.
