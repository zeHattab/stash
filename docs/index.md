# Stash

**Offline, open-source password & document manager for iPhone.** No account, no
internet, no tracking. Your data is encrypted on the device and never leaves it.

- Passwords, logins, a strong generator and password history
- Two-factor codes (TOTP) with AutoFill in Safari and apps
- Documents with an on-device passport (MRZ) scanner and expiry reminders
- A recovery key and a second (decoy) password for protection under pressure
- Free, no ads, no in-app purchases — open source under GPLv3
